Adding, using and removing ZoomLens

Last updated October 29, 2026

What you need

  • The Zoom desktop client, version 6.5.5 or later.
  • A meeting whose host allows apps to access meeting content. Hosts find this in their Zoom web settings.

Add it

  1. Find ZoomLens in the Zoom App Marketplace and choose Add.
  2. Review what it asks for and choose Allow. A page confirms that ZoomLens is added to your account.

Start it in a meeting

  1. In a meeting, open Apps in the toolbar and choose ZoomLens. The panel opens beside the meeting.
  2. If it says Not started, press Start ZoomLens in this meeting. If you're the host it starts straight away; otherwise Zoom asks the host to allow it.
  3. Once it says Ready, it can see whatever is being shared.

Ask about the shared screen

  • Describe screen gives a short summary of what's shared.
  • Explain says what it means, not just what it shows.
  • What changed? catches you up on what's new since your last answer.
  • Type your own question in the box at the bottom, and keep asking: a follow-up builds on the answer before.

Answers appear only in your panel. Nobody else in the meeting sees them, including the person sharing.

Accessibility modes

  • Announce changes describes the shared screen whenever it changes in a way worth mentioning, so a screen reader reads it out without you asking.
  • Color Assist names things by label rather than color, adds a note when a screen shows something only by color, and adds an Explain colors button. It stays on in your next meeting.

Both are off until you turn them on, and only affect your own panel.

For hosts

If you started ZoomLens as the host, your panel has an Allow ZoomLens here switch. Turning it off stops ZoomLens for everyone in the meeting, and their panels say the host turned it off. Turn it back on whenever you like.

Remove it

In the Zoom App Marketplace, open the apps you've added, find ZoomLens and choose Remove. ZoomLens keeps no account data, so nothing else needs deleting. What it does with meeting content is in the Privacy Policy.
